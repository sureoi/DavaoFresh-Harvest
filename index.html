Our Roots • about.html

Bridging Mindanao's Highland Farms & Davao Kitchens

Founded in Tagum City, DavaoFresh Harvest started with a simple mission: ensure smallholder vegetable growers in Marilog District, Kapatagan, and Davao del Norte receive fair, transparent farm-gate prices while giving local households access to truly fresh produce.

Traditional supply chains pass vegetables through up to four middlemen, adding days of transit and heat exposure. With our digital harvest-on-demand system, your order triggers early-morning harvesting and direct cold-crate sorting at our Apokon hub.
